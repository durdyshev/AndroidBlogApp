# Privacy Policy for Aura Dating & Social Discovery

**Last Updated:** September 5, 2026

Welcome to **Aura** ("Aura", "we", "our", or "us"). Aura is a dating and social discovery mobile application designed to help people connect, chat, and form meaningful relationships.

Your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use the Aura mobile application and associated services (the "Service").

---

## 1. Age Requirement (Strictly 18+)

Aura is strictly intended for individuals who are **18 years of age or older**. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that a person under 18 has created an account, we will immediately delete their account and associated data.

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide Directly
- **Profile Information:** When registering and setting up your profile, you provide your phone number/email, display name, date of birth, gender, bio, and dating preferences (age range, preferred gender).
- **Photos & Media:** Photos you upload to your profile or send in private chats.
- **Interests & Lifestyle Badges:** Interests and hobbies you select to personalize your profile.
- **Communications:** Messages, media, and interactions you send to other users within the app.
- **Customer Support & Reports:** Information you provide when submitting reports, blocking users, or contacting our support team.

### B. Information Collected Automatically
- **Location Data:** With your explicit permission, we collect your device's geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude) to calculate approximate distances between you and other candidates and enable location-based discovery. You can control or revoke location permissions at any time in your device settings.
- **Device & Technical Information:** IP address, device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, language settings, and network state.
- **Usage Activity:** Interaction timestamps, swipe history, active/online status, and app performance logs.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data to:
1. Create and manage your account and profile.
2. Provide core matchmaking, location filtering, and real-time chat features.
3. Calculate approximate distances to nearby matches (we never share your exact GPS coordinates with other users).
4. Enforce community guidelines, detect spam, prevent fraud, and moderate inappropriate user-generated content (UGC).
5. Send push notifications regarding new matches, likes, and messages (manageable via in-app settings).
6. Improve and optimize app performance and reliability.

---

## 4. How Your Information is Shared

We **never sell or rent** your personal data to third parties. Your information is only shared under the following conditions:
- **With Other Users:** Your public profile details (display name, photos, age, bio, interests, approximate distance in km, and online status if enabled) are visible to potential matches.
- **Service Providers & Infrastructure:** We use trusted cloud infrastructure providers (such as Supabase for secure database and authentication, and Google Firebase for push notifications).
- **Legal Compliance:** When required by applicable law, court order, or governmental authority to protect safety, investigate fraud, or prevent illegal activity.

---

## 5. User Privacy Controls & Choices

Aura provides granular in-app controls to manage your privacy:
- **Show/Hide Distance:** Option to hide distance calculations from other users.
- **Show/Hide Online Status:** Option to toggle your active/online indicator on or off.
- **Blocking & Reporting:** Ability to immediately block and report any user. Blocked users cannot see your profile or send messages.
- **Edit / Update Data:** You can modify your photos, bio, and preferences anytime in your Profile screen.

---

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain your personal information only for as long as your account is active or as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy.

### How to Delete Your Account:
1. **In-App Deletion:** Open Aura > Settings > Account > **Delete Account**. Confirming this action will immediately anonymize and permanently delete your profile, photos, matches, and message history from our servers.
2. **Web Deletion Request:** If you have uninstalled the app or cannot log in, you can request account deletion via our web portal or by emailing **privacy@auraapp.com** with your registered phone number/email.

---

## 7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (SSL/TLS for data in transit, encrypted storage, and row-level database security policies) to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, loss, or alteration.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
