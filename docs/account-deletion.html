Account & Data Deletion Request

Delete your Aura account and associated personal information

Permanent Action: Deleting your account is irreversible. Your profile, photos, matches, swipe history, and all chat conversations will be permanently deleted.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

1 Open the Aura app on your device.

2 Go to your Profile tab > tap Settings (Gear icon).

3 Tap on Account Settings > select Delete Account.

4 Confirm deletion. Your data will be wiped immediately.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request (If App is Uninstalled)

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your account, submit a deletion request below or email privacy@auraapp.com:

What Data is Deleted?

  • Immediately Deleted: Profile photo files, display name, bio, birth date, preferences, swipes, matches, and message text.
  • Retention Period: All data is permanently purged within 30 days of request.
