Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 5, 2026
Strictly 18+ Only

Welcome to Aura Dating & Social Discovery ("Aura", "we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy and safety are our top priorities. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Age Requirement

Strictly 18+: Aura is exclusively intended for adults aged 18 and older. We do not allow individuals under 18 to register or use our platform.

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide

  • Account & Profile: Phone number or email, display name, date of birth, gender, bio, and dating preferences.
  • Photos & Media: Profile photos uploaded to showcase your personality and media shared in private conversations.
  • Interests: Lifestyle tags, hobbies, and interest categories you select.
  • Messages: Chat communications sent to other matched users.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Location Data: With your permission, we collect precise/approximate GPS coordinates to compute distances in kilometers and enable city/region searches. We never reveal your exact street address or raw coordinates to other users.
  • Device & Activity: Device model, OS version, active online status, and crash logs.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To match you with compatible people nearby or in your chosen location.
  • To facilitate secure, real-time messaging between mutual matches.
  • To enforce community safety, detect spam, prevent fraud, and moderate inappropriate content.
  • To deliver push notifications regarding likes, matches, and incoming messages.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We never sell your personal data. We only share information with trusted infrastructure partners essential for operating the app (e.g., Supabase for database security and Google Firebase for notifications).

5. Your Privacy Controls

  • Hide Online Status: Toggle your active indicator on/off in Settings.
  • Hide Distance: Choose whether other users see your distance.
  • Block & Report: Instantly block or report any profile. Blocked users cannot interact with you.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You can permanently delete your account and all associated messages, photos, and match history at any time:

  • In-App: Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • Web Request: Visit our Account Deletion Page or email privacy@auraapp.com.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our Data Protection Team at privacy@auraapp.com.
